Everyday examples

Where the time
usually goes.

These are patterns I see in small businesses. Your version may look different. We start from your week, not from a menu of software.

01 / HAND-OFFS

The same details are typed again so the next person can start.

A customer says yes in an email. Someone then retypes the name, the address, and what they bought into the sheet or the job list the rest of the team uses.

One agreed place for the details.
02 / FOLLOW-THROUGH

The next step lives in someone’s memory.

A quote, a renewal, or a callback is due. Nothing on the calendar says so. The only system is remembering to look, and some of those people never hear back.

A visible next step, while you still decide what to say.
03 / SETTING UP

Starting the work takes longer than the work.

Before anyone can begin, the same folders, notes, and messages have to be created. Miss one, and the job starts with a scramble.

The usual setup happens the same way each time.

Examples of repeated work, not a list of products. What we change depends on how your team already works.
