Everyday examples

Reports that cost
a morning.

The useful change is usually smaller than a new reporting system. It is getting the same agreed figures together without rebuilding the layout from scratch.

01 / GATHERING

The week starts by collecting last week’s numbers.

Someone copies figures out of the sales tool, the bank, a spreadsheet, and a few emails before the update is fit to send. The report is mostly assembly.

The sources gathered once, in an agreed layout.
02 / ONE PERSON

Only one person knows which number is the real one.

The sheet looks finished, but a few rows are estimates, a few are adjusted, and the method lives in that person’s head. A vacation delays the update.

The routine figures prepared the same way each time.
03 / A NEW CUT

A small question rebuilds the whole report.

Someone asks for the same figures by location, or by month, and the layout starts over. The numbers did not change. The arrangement did.

More than one view of figures you already trust.

You still read the result and decide what it means. The copying and reformatting take less of the week.
