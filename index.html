Problems I've Helped Solve

Recovering Years of Business Data

A business had years of customer and transaction records trapped in outdated software. The information was still there, but it could not be opened or used with everyday tools. I developed a solution to recover and organize those records so the business could access them and work with them in modern software.

Turning Old Documents into Usable Data

Important business records were stored across years of PDF documents. The details were useful, but finding them meant reading through files one by one. I built a process to extract and organize the information, making it easier to search, analyze, and reuse without manually reviewing every document.

Automating Recruitment Workflows with AI

Recruitment involved repetitive work: collecting information, keeping it organized, and moving it between the tools the business already used. I developed an automation solution that connects those existing tools with AI, helping organize the information and streamline the repetitive tasks.

Simplifying Complex Business Calculations

Some work depends on calculations that are easy to get wrong when they are done by hand, especially across a large batch of records. I worked on specialized software that automates those calculations, applies the business rules, and processes the information consistently.
