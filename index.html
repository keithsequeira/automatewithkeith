Solutions for everyday operations

Where is manual work
holding you back?

Not sure where to start? That’s okay. These are just a few ways I help businesses work smarter. Often, the biggest opportunities come from simply talking through how things work today.

01 / INQUIRIES

“We copy every inquiry into a spreadsheet.”

Capture details from your inquiry form, add them to your records, and alert the person who needs to respond.

Less copying. Clearer ownership.
02 / FOLLOW-UPS

“Good leads go quiet. We forget to chase.”

Create timely reminders for open quotes and inquiries, so your team knows who needs a follow-up.

Keep the next step in sight.
03 / REPORTING

“Friday disappears into building reports.”

Bring agreed figures into one scheduled report, without gathering and reformatting them from scratch.

Spend more time using the numbers.
04 / DOCUMENTS

“Someone has to type all this into Excel.”

Extract selected details from documents into a usable table, with uncertain entries flagged for checking.

Less typing. Easier review.
05 / ONBOARDING

“Every new customer means the same admin.”

Set up folders, send welcome information, and assign the right tasks when a new customer comes on board.

A more organized first impression.
06 / DISCONNECTED TOOLS

“We enter the same details in three places.”

Connect compatible tools so agreed information moves between them, with checks when something needs attention.

Fewer handovers. Less duplicate work.

Examples only. Your own work may suggest something else.
