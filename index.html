Real Problems. Practical Solutions.

E&E Gliddon · Legacy data conversion

Customer records recovered from an older system

E&E Gliddon, a service company, had decades of customer and invoice records stored in a desktop application from the 1990s. The files used a proprietary .DTA format that Excel and other everyday tools could not open.

I recovered the records from those files and delivered them as CSV spreadsheets. Customer names, phone numbers, and addresses were separated into columns, along with a company name when the original file included one. The business could open the spreadsheets and use them for reporting or a later move to newer software.
